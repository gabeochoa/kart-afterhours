Cart Chaos

High-octane couch chaos: strap weapons to a car, grab your friends, and last one rolling wins.

Cart Chaos is a fast, local-multiplayer vehicular combat game for up to 8 players. Drift, boost, and blast your way around a wrap-around arena — solo against AI or in a full living-room brawl.

Weaponized carnage

Every car comes armed. Fire left or right relative to where you're pointed, boost to chase or escape, and use the arena's wrap-around edges to land shots your rivals never see coming. Master the drift to keep your momentum through tight turns.

Four ways to play

  • Lives — everyone gets a set number of lives; last car standing wins.
  • Kills — timed deathmatch; most eliminations when the clock runs out takes it.
  • Hippo — race to scoop up spawning items before anyone else hits the target.
  • Tag and Go — nobody wants to be "IT"; it slows you down, so pass it on fast.

Tune the match

Set it up your way before the green light: pick the mode, flip on Team A vs Team B, choose a time limit, toggle which weapons are in play, and set starting lives.

Play your way

Keyboard or gamepad, solo or up to 8 local players. Grab whoever's on the couch and settle it.

Features

  • Local multiplayer for up to 8 players
  • Vehicular combat with drifting, boosting, and directional weapons
  • Four game modes plus team play
  • Wrap-around arena you can shoot across
  • Solo mode against AI for practice
  • Full keyboard and gamepad support

Tagline: Weaponized karts, 8-player couch chaos, last one rolling wins.
